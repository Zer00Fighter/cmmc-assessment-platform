# Sprint 13 — Multi-framework harmonization and assessment reuse

Sprint 13 operationalizes framework mappings inside an assessment while preserving independent compliance conclusions.

## Operating model

- An assessment may contain one or many frameworks representing one or many compliance needs.
- The **Omni Control Framework** is explicitly designated as Omni's native mapping hub.
- Direct mappings are preferred. Omni may derive a candidate relationship between two external frameworks through a shared Omni control.
- Derived relationships are suggestions, not compliance determinations.
- Assessors approve or reject reuse and record a rationale.
- Approval can share accepted evidence and recognize prior testing by reference.
- Omni never copies a control or objective outcome through a mapping.
- Every decision retains its direct or Omni-derived basis and mapping path for auditability.
- Retained Omni mapping references are re-resolved whenever another framework is approved, so framework import order does not discard future relationships.

## Workflow

Open an assessment and choose **Harmonize**. Run mapping analysis, review each suggested relationship, choose whether evidence and testing may be reused, document the rationale, and approve or reject it. Framework dashboards and reports continue to use their own control results.

Private framework sources, client evidence, and organization records remain runtime data and are never committed.
